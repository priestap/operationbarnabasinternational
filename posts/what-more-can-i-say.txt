God is good… all the time!

<h3>How can I say “thanks” for all that He has done?</h3>

He has kept His promises.<br>He has provided His grace in abundance.<br>He has given security.<br>He has anointed and filled with His Spirit.<br>He has answered prayer.<br>He has encouraged so many of His chosen shepherds.

His mercy is new every morning.<br>His grace is sufficient for every situation.<br>His peace calms our troubled hearts.<br>His joy cannot be stated with mere words.<br>His faithfulness is great.<br>His love is everlasting.

Join me in a spirit of praise and gratitude to Him who was, who is, and is to come!

Your prayers… your encouragement… your investment… all work together to provide encouragement to some of the most important people on earth… the shepherds, chosen of God to feed and lead His flock! Thank you!

Privileged by grace; committed to serve,<br>Jerry
