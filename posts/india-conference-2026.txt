title: Two Conferences for Pastors in India
date: 2026-11-05
category: News
---
This fall, OBI held two conferences for pastors in India, the first for about 50 pastors and the second for about 60. It was OBI’s sixth overseas trip of 2026, a year that has also taken ministry teams to Cambodia, Vietnam, Honduras, and Tanzania.

Through the prayers and gifts of OBI’s partners, hundreds of pastors each year are renewed in their calling and rededicate themselves to shepherding God’s flock.
