title: A New OBI Team in Tanzania
date: 2026-10-12
category: News
---
In September, at the invitation of a Tanzanian mission leader, OBI trained five leaders to conduct OBI conferences for pastors in their own country, where many pastors are deeply discouraged.

In October the new team held its first conference, for more than 50 leaders, and reported great excitement at what God was doing among them.

The Tanzanian team joins the Kenyan OBI team, which has held three to four conferences a year since its training in 2019.
