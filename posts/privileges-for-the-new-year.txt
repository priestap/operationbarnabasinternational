Move ahead with confidence…

<strong>The manger is empty…</strong><br>…at the perfect time, God sent us His Son. That is history!

<strong>The cross is empty…</strong><br>…the price for our redemption has been paid. That also is history!

<strong>The tomb is empty…</strong><br>…providing victory over sin, death, and hell. That too is history!

We move into the new year with confidence because of our privileges!

<strong>The privilege of prayer…</strong><br>…He knows, He understands, He intercedes! (Hebrews 4:15–16)

<strong>The privilege of fellowship…</strong><br>…meeting together to encourage one another! (Hebrews 10:25)

<strong>The privilege of fullness…</strong><br>…the Spirit within, assuring, leading, gifting, using, and flowing out! (John 7:37–38; Acts 1:8; Ephesians 5:18; Galatians 5:16, 22–23)

<h3>A short anecdote for your edification</h3>

In December OBI sent some money to provide a leader in Africa the privilege of bringing his pastors and their wives together for a one-day, end-of-year gathering of good food, warm fellowship, and the ministry of the Word… a “mini-OBI”! I just got the report of joy, testimonies, and tears… one pastor said it had been a long time since he and his wife were able to have a good meal together! That’s encouragement… that’s OBI!

I wish each of you a blessed new year! Let’s keep going! Crowning day is coming!

Privileged by grace; committed to serve,<br>Jerry
