title: Ministry in South Korea and the Philippines
date: 2025-10-12
category: News
---
In October, OBI ministered in South Korea and the Philippines.

In South Korea, OBI led a weekend Spiritual Life Conference for 40 Nepali and Filipino university students and young adults, with four services. There is a real move of God among college and career-age young people in Korea, as there is in the United States.

In the Philippines, OBI spoke at a retreat for 50 pastors and a group of their wives, ministered in a local church’s Sunday services, and then held an OBI conference for 126 pastors and wives. Though the country was shaken by a series of earthquakes that week, the meetings went ahead without disruption.

Across both countries, the Word was shared eleven times in the first days alone, with many personal commitments, testimonies, and times of prayer. OBI’s part is to be available and faithful; God does the work in people’s hearts, and eternity will reveal the results.
