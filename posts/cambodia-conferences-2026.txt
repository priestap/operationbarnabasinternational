title: Two Conferences in Cambodia
date: 2026-01-20
category: News
---
In January, an OBI ministry team held two conferences in Cambodia, each for about 40 pastors. The theme was “Refreshing Tired Feet,” drawn from Romans 10:15.

The team asked OBI’s partners to pray that each gathering would be a “holy convocation” marked by the Lord’s presence, depending on His favor, protection, guidance, and anointing.
