A Conference for Pastors in India

News October 2026

In October, OBI held a conference for 50 pastors in India. It was OBI’s sixth overseas trip of 2026, a year that has also taken ministry teams to Cambodia, Vietnam, Honduras, and Tanzania.

Through the prayers and gifts of OBI’s partners, hundreds of pastors each year are renewed in their calling and rededicate themselves to shepherding God’s flock.
